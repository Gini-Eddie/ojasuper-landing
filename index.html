Why OjaSuper?

Engineered to solve the realities of high-friction, low-connectivity retail workflows.

🎙️

Voice-First Interaction

Hands busy unpacking goods or serving customers? Speak naturally: "We restocked 50 cartons of milk" and let OjaSuper parse the intent.

⚡

Zero Cloud Dependency

Powered by local Whisper STT models and FastAPI. It operates flawlessly during power outages, server downtimes, or in dead zones.

🛡️

Autonomous Safeguards

Built-in Business Rules Engine prevents negative stock, alerts on low margins, and auto-creates missing inventory items on the fly.

📊

Real-Time Ledger

Every transaction instantly updates your gross profit, total revenue, and inventory count on a dynamic Single Page Application.

⌨️

Fallback Text Input

In a noisy market? Instantly switch from voice recording to typing text commands through the same intelligent AI pipeline.

📱

Cross-Device Sync

Run the backend securely on a local shop laptop, and access the fast, responsive dashboard from any smartphone connected to the shop's WiFi.

Under the Hood

A 5-stage offline pipeline turning raw speech into verified database ledger actions in milliseconds.

1

Audio Capture

Browser records audio via WebRTC and sends the blob to FastAPI.

2

Whisper STT

Local AI model converts the raw audio payload into highly accurate text.

3

LLM Extraction

Small language model identifies intent (Sale, Restock) and parameters.

4

Rules Engine

Validates stock logic, price overrides, and fuzzy-matches item names.

5

SQLite Action

Pydantic schemas execute CRUD operations and log audit trails securely.
